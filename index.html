Your trusted local butcher

Raised right, packed right

We started Montana City Meats in the fall of 2002 with a passion for raising our own locally grown animals. All of our products are locally raised and slaughtered without hormones or other chemicals.

Our animals are finished on grass and alfalfa, with high quality grain in the mornings and evenings. Every order is cut to your instructions and sealed in premium CryoVak shrink packaging.

Domestic processing & slaughter

Book your processing slot

  1. Pick your animal type and preferred drop-off date in our online scheduler.
  2. Pay a non-refundable deposit per animal to hold your spot. It's applied to your final bill.
  3. We confirm, or call you to reschedule if that day is full. Up to 10 animals per booking; call for more.
  4. Get every cut back vacuum packed with your name on each package. We guarantee you get all your own meat back.

Brand inspections are required for all cattle brought in for processing.
